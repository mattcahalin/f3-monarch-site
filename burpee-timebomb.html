    How to Play

    1. Circle up the PAX. One phone, screen on, sound up.
    2. Tap Light the Fuse. The timer is random and hidden.
    3. While it burns, everyone does the exercise on the screen AMRAP until the bomb comes to them.
    4. Whoever receives the bomb must do at least 2 reps of that exercise (the hard way where applicable) before passing it to the next PAX. If the fuse exercise is turned off, it's at least 2 burpees.
    5. Pass the phone hand to hand. No tossing (it's somebody's phone).
    6. When it blows, the PAX holding it reads the task out loud. Everyone does it.
    7. Tap Next Round. The unlucky PAX lights the next fuse.
