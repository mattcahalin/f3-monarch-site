    How to Play

    1. Circle up the PAX. One phone, screen on, sound up.
    2. Tap Light the Fuse. The timer is random and hidden.
    3. Pass the phone hand to hand. No holding, no tossing (it's somebody's phone).
    4. While it burns, everyone does the exercise on the screen. Keep going until it blows.
    5. When it blows, the PAX holding it reads the task out loud. Everyone does it.
    6. Tap Next Round. The unlucky PAX lights the next fuse.
