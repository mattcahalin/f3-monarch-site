July 2026 — First Spotlight

Porky

The M.A.C.E. (man achieving community excellence) has officially been handed off to @Porky. In the year-ish (?) since he's joined F3, he has become a Monarch staple, showing up 5x days a week at the trap and the asylum, taking on Q's, putting in the work, and driving his partners harder. Whereas others are more vocal cheerleaders, he (like many others in Monarch) pushes himself and others with a quiet tenacity that sets the example to follow. Congratulations @Porky and continue inspiring us all!
