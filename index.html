The 3 F's

F3 stands on three core pillars. Everything we do flows from these.

Fitness

Free, intense, peer-led workouts every morning. Every workout is functional, scalable, and led by a different man each time. No two are alike.

Fellowship

Men holding each other accountable, on the field and off. The sweat fades, the bonds don't. This is where real friendships are built.

Faith

A calling to lead and serve — in our homes, workplaces, and communities. F3 is faith-friendly to all men, regardless of background or belief.

Neighboring Regions

Core & West County

F3 Saint Louis (The OG Region)

The OG for Missouri, the Saint Louis region has grown from 5 PAX to well into the four-figure range since 2017. Starting out with just one AO (“The Huck” in Clayton), the Saint Louis area just keeps experiencing explosive growth, as seen by all the regions that have spun off in the past few years.

f3stlouis.com →

F3 Spirit of St. Louis (SoSL)

The Outer Belt region of St. Louis County, F3 SoSL serves the PAX West of Interstate 270 up to the borders of JeffCo and St. Charles. From Fenton to Chesterfield to Pacific, if you are looking for a great workout in the Westward Expansion of St. Louis, this is your place.

facebook.com/f3spiritstl →

City & South

F3 City of St. Louis

In July 2022, we continued our expansion through our starfish model by establishing the City of St. Louis region, which includes all AOs inside St. Louis City limits as well as our Illinois AOs.

F3 Jefferson County (JeffCo)
